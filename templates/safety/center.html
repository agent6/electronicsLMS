Safety Center

ObsoleteHQ keeps hardware learning low-voltage, clear, and beginner-safe without making it scary.

3.3V GPIO

Pico 2 W GPIO is 3.3V. Do not feed 5V signals into GPIO pins.

Motors and pumps

Do not power motors, pumps, or relays directly from GPIO. Use drivers and external power.

Relays

Beginner relay projects stay low-voltage DC only.

Lasers and flame sensors

Use eye safety for lasers and never create unsafe fire activities.

Water and batteries

Keep water controlled and treat LiPo charging as careful supervised work.

Responsible sharing

Use display names and avoid personal details in public project text.
