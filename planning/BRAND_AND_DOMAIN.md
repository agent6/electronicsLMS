# Brand and Domain

## Brand

Product name: ObsoleteHQ.

Domain: `obsoletehq.com`.

Brand promise: learn electronics by building real things, from one blinking LED to student-choice inventions.

## Positioning

ObsoleteHQ makes old barriers feel outdated: electronics is too hard, hardware is only for experts, code has to stay on a screen, and getting stuck means failing.

Do not overuse the word obsolete. Use it as a clever idea, not as a negative drumbeat.

## Tagline Directions

- Learn electronics by building real things.
- Start with one blinking LED. End with your own invention.
- Turn parts into projects.
- From blink to your own invention.
- Make boring tutorials obsolete.

## Voice

Energetic but clear. The copy should sound like a capable teen maker lab: direct, vivid, safe, and project-first.

## Homepage

The homepage should speak directly to students. It should explain what ObsoleteHQ is, what students build, how the recommended kits help, how progress works, and why public writeups use display names only.

Primary CTA: Start Learning.

## SEO

Suggested titles:

- ObsoleteHQ - Learn Electronics with MicroPython and Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W
- ObsoleteHQ - Teen Electronics Projects with MicroPython
- ObsoleteHQ - From Blink to Your Own Invention

## Naming Rules

Use ObsoleteHQ as the only product/platform name. Do not introduce a second curriculum name or sub-brand.
