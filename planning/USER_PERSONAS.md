# User Personas

## Absolute Beginner Student

Goals: make a first circuit work, understand what the Pico 2 W is doing, and stop feeling intimidated by electronics.

Fears: breaking the board, wiring something wrong, not understanding errors, and looking lost.

Needs: tiny first steps, safety reminders, clear part lists, visible results, and debugging help by symptom.

Helpful features: setup tutorials, blink path, wiring checklists, Dev Log prompts, private progress, and simple next-step recommendations.

## Coding-Experienced Student

Goals: connect code to real hardware, move quickly through basics, and build projects that react to the world.

Fears: being slowed down by overly basic explanations or locked paths.

Needs: fast scanning, optional deeper explanations, open project choice, and clear prerequisites.

Helpful features: tutorial filters, parts-based metadata, recommended next lessons, and student-choice projects.

## Fast-Moving Self-Paced Student

Goals: move through published tutorials quickly and choose harder projects when ready.

Fears: artificial locks and forced pacing.

Needs: freedom to browse any published project, visible skill growth, and remixes.

Helpful features: A la Carte library, skill tags, published-only navigation, XP, badges, and project writeups.

## Limited-Time Student

Goals: make progress during short sessions and resume without penalty.

Fears: losing streaks, forgetting where they were, and falling behind a suggested path.

Needs: resume state, meaningful-session momentum, private notes, and no shame for gaps.

Helpful features: dashboard resume card, Dev Logs, comeback copy, non-punitive momentum, and small tutorial checkpoints.

## Privacy-Conscious Student

Goals: share what they built without exposing personal details.

Fears: accidentally sharing real identity or contact information.

Needs: display name, clear publish UI, no media upload requirement, and fields that never ask for sensitive data.

Helpful features: private Dev Logs, public project writeups, display-name-only attribution, and email verification before publishing.

## Admin / Content Operator

Goals: publish complete lessons, keep hidden drafts out of student UI, manage user/account basics, and monitor operations.

Fears: accidentally publishing incomplete content, broken links, unsafe wording, or operational blind spots.

Needs: clean custom dashboard, draft/published states, content previews, link checks, analytics basics, email status, and health checks.

Helpful features: admin content lists, publish validation, route visibility controls, operation panels, and QA checklists.
