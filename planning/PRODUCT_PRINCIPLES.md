# Product Principles

## Learning Principles

- Build first, explain second.
- Start with blink.
- Add one concept at a time.
- Every new sensor starts with raw values.
- Every output starts with the simplest possible action.
- Every larger project combines known skills.
- Every student-choice project should feel like ownership, not compliance.
- Debugging is normal and documented.
- Creativity matters more than quiz scores.
- Completion should mean confidence.

## Product Principles

- Self-paced by default.
- Published content only appears when it works.
- Hidden content stays out of student navigation.
- Fully working beats visually fake.
- No dead buttons.
- No dead links.
- No empty active pages.
- No fake progress, fake notifications, or fake achievements.
- Mobile and desktop are equal citizens.
- Polished does not mean complicated.
- Reduce cognitive load.

## Page Questions

Every student-facing page should answer:

- Where am I?
- What can I do next?
- What did I complete?
- What part do I need?
- What should I check before powering it?
- Where do I go if I am stuck?

## Tone Principles

- Energetic but clear.
- Teen-first, not childish.
- Maker-lab focused.
- Concrete over motivational filler.
- Encourage persistence without pressure.
- Celebrate working projects, thoughtful debugging, and useful remixes.
